About Us

What We Do

Walker Street Ventures is a real estate investment firm focused on acquiring shallow bay industrial and unanchored strip retail properties along the East Coast of the United States.

We seek opportunities in markets where supply is constrained and demand from small-to-mid-size tenants remains durable — asset classes that have historically offered resilient cash flows and favorable risk-adjusted returns.

Shallow Bay Industrial

Last-mile and infill industrial facilities serving local and regional tenants. Typically single-story, multi-tenant buildings in supply-constrained East Coast submarkets.

Unanchored Strip Retail

Neighborhood-serving retail centers without a major anchor tenant. We target well-located, necessity-based strips with stable tenancy and value-add potential.

Geographic focus: East Coast, United States
