About Us

What We Do

Walker Street Ventures is a real estate investment firm focused on opportunistic acquisitions along the East Coast of the United States.

We pursue creative, off-market opportunities where we can apply a differentiated lens — targeting situations overlooked by larger capital sources and creating value through hands-on asset management and flexible deal structuring.

Our People

Zachary Janfaza

Managing Partner

Zachary is the founder of Walker Street Ventures. He spent nearly five years on the investments team at Oxford Properties Group in New York, where he completed over $4 billion of transactions across asset classes.

Prior to Oxford, Zachary gained experience in acquisitions at SL Green Realty Corp. and The Davis Companies across New York and Boston.

Zachary holds an A.B. in Government with a secondary in Economics from Harvard University.
